Run-Time Estimator

A rough guide to how long a uniform-mesh run takes, from the cost per cell and stage measured on the workstation of this page (Threadripper PRO 5965WX, 1, 16 and 48 threads, 2048² to 4096² and 256³ meshes): time = cells × stages × steps × cost. It scales the numbers of one machine and one set of cases, so treat it as an order of magnitude (the cost per cell is higher on small meshes that fit in the cache, and 3-D costs more per cell than 2-D).
