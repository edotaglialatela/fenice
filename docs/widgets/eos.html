Equation of State Explorer

Pressure and speed of sound against the internal energy per unit volume, $\rho e_{\rm int}$, at fixed density, for the two equations of state of the solver: ideal gas $p = (\gamma-1)\rho e_{\rm int}$ and stiffened gas $p + p_\infty = (\gamma-1)\rho e_{\rm int}$.

Non-dimensional units. The solver defaults are γ = 1.4 (air) and, for water, γ = 4.4 with p∞ = 6×108 Pa; the slider values here only show the trend.
